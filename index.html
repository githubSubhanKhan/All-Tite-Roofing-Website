Questions people ask before they call

Still unsure? Call and ask. Even if we aren't your final choice, we'll help you know what to look for in a roofer.

How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?

Missing or cracked shingles, granules in the gutters, sagging sections, active leaks, or a roof older than about 20 years are the usual signs. Our free inspection tells you honestly which one you need.

Do you work with insurance companies?

Yes. We have long experience with insurance claims and will help you through the process for storm-related repairs or replacement.

Are you insured for roof work specifically?

Yes — fully licensed and insured for roofing. Whoever you hire, make sure their policy actually covers roof work.

How long should a new roof last?

Asphalt shingles typically last 20–30 years, metal 50+ and slate even longer. Installation quality and regular maintenance make the biggest difference.

Will you make a mess of my property?

It's the most common worry, and we take it seriously. We protect what's around the house during tear-off and put back anything we had to move.

Are estimates really free?

Yes. Every estimate is free and comes with no obligation.
